# Hi, I'm Abdel 👋

Final-year **Mechatronics & Embedded Systems** engineering student at **ESIX Normandie** (Université de Caen Normandie).
Passionate about **Embedded Linux** and **RTOS**: I like working close to the hardware, from bare-metal firmware on STM32 to custom Linux images, and up to ROS2 pipelines on autonomous vehicles.

- 🐧 **Embedded Linux**: Yocto, Buildroot, kernel fundamentals, cross-compilation toolchains
- ⏱️ **RTOS**: FreeRTOS (CMSIS-RTOS v1/v2), currently diving into **Zephyr RTOS**
- 🔌 **Bare-metal**: STM32 HAL/LL and register-level programming, DMA, interrupts, timers, bootloader & flash
- 🚗 **Automotive & robotics**: CAN bus, radar perception, ROS2 on autonomous vehicles
- 🤝 Open to **open-source contributions** and Embedded Linux / RTOS / automotive opportunities
